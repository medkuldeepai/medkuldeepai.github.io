# Interstitial Lung Disease Bioinformatics Analysis

Network-based bioinformatics analysis to identify genes involved in lung fibrosis and chronic hypersensitivity pneumonitis.

## Tools

- Cytoscape
- CytoHubba
- STRING
- R

## Findings

Identified 12 key candidate genes associated with fibrosis pathways.
